My *Intro to Animation Demo Reel* is a collection of various animations made with different techniques and software. The animations showcase a range of styles and methods, providing a comprehensive overview of my ability to learn and experiment in the field of animation. The demo reel includes animations created using rotoscoping, 3D modeling and animation, and procedural animation techniques. Each animation demonstrates a different aspect of my abilities, from character animation to environmental design.
